# CyberShield — Brand Guidelines & Identity

This document defines the brand voice, visual personality, iconography, illustration style, and AI assistant character for CyberShield.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Vision

CyberShield stands for **clarity, vigilance, autonomy, and zero-compromise security**. The platform is designed to look like the command bridge of a futuristic cybersecurity operation—clean, authoritative, ultra-modern, yet accessible to human operators.

---

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone

- **Authoritative & Trustworthy**: Clear, precise terminology without hyperbole.
- **Empowering & Action-Oriented**: Provides direct remediation steps rather than vague alerts.
- **Plain-English Explanations ("ELI5")**: Always accompanies deep technical telemetry (e.g. SHA-256, MITRE T1059) with intuitive human summaries.
- **Calm Under Pressure**: When threats occur, the UI transitions to high-contrast warning/threat colors without inducing panic, focusing immediately on containment actions.

---

## 3. Iconography & Visual Assets

- **Primary Icon Library**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Stroke Width**: `1.75px` to `2.0px` for UI clarity and consistency.
- **Icon Enclosures**: Soft square or circular containers with subtle accent backgrounds (`bg-sky-500/10` or `bg-emerald-500/10`).
- **Logo Mark**: Concentric polygonal cyber shield with an electrified central node emitting soft radial light.

---

## 4. AI Companion Personality ("Sentinel AI")

- **Name**: Sentinel AI / CyberShield Copilot
- **Avatar**: Glowing cyan/emerald holographic core.
- **Behavior**: Proactive threat detection, interactive vulnerability querying, automated CLI script generation.
- **Confidence Rating**: Every response includes an AI confidence score (e.g., "High Confidence — 99.4%").

---

## 5. Empty State & Illustration Style

- Use minimalist geometric SVG vector outlines with soft radial glow gradients.
- Avoid cluttered stock imagery or generic cartoons.
- Always include an affirmative status message (e.g., *"All systems nominal — zero perimeter anomalies detected"*) and an explicit action button.
